Mark prices come from Binance USDT-margined futures, with Bybit, OKX and then CoinGecko spot as backups. They refresh every 30 seconds while this page is open.

Balance = starting balance + realised P&L + open P&L. Period gains count trades by their close time; open P&L is counted in the current period. P&L % is the price move times leverage (the return on margin), or the plain price move when no leverage is recorded. Times are shown in your local time zone; weeks start on Monday.
